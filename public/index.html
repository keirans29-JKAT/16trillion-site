How we're paid

Who pays your adviser decides whose side they're on.

Most mutual fund recommendations in India come from distributors, paid a commission by the fund house. We are paid only by the families we advise.

Commission-based distributor

  • Paid by the fund house, out of your returns, every year
  • Regular plans, with higher expense ratios
  • Earns more when you hold the funds that pay more

16T₹illion, fee-only

  • Paid only by you, under a signed advisory agreement
  • Direct plans, so more of the return stays in your portfolio
  • No reason to prefer one fund house over another

Before you ask

Honest answers, upfront.

Is there a minimum amount to start?+

No. The discovery call is free and open to everyone. Advice should scale to the person, not the corpus.

How do you charge?+

Only through a fee you agree to in a signed advisory agreement, disclosed in full before you commit. We accept no commission from any fund house. [FEE DETAILS]

Do you sell mutual funds or insurance?+

No. We advise; we don't distribute. We recommend direct plans, and every recommendation comes with its reasoning in writing.

Are you a SEBI-registered investment adviser?+

Yes. 16 Trillion Wealth Advisory LLP is registered with SEBI as an Investment Adviser, registration no. [SEBI RIA REG. NO.]. Our registration details and disclosures are listed at the foot of every page.
